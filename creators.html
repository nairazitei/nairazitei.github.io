Pricing creators on proof, not on follower count

Lumiar Agency · Brand Partnerships Manager (freelance) · 2025–present

3x rate per sponsored post BRL 1K → 3K+

The problem

Two Brazilian content creators, one in the pet niche and one in sports, had growing audiences but were charging around BRL 1K per sponsored post. They had no structured way to approach brands, and no argument for asking more.

The goal

Reposition the creators in the market, so brands would pay for their real value.

What I noticed

The creators were waiting to be found. They rarely approached brands, and their media kits didn't show, in numbers, how many people they could reach. When they did reach out, the messages were generic, with no angle that made sense for each brand.

What I did

  1. Priced on performance

    I set rates from each creator's real metrics and updated their media kits every month with audience and engagement data.

  2. Went to the brands

    I built prospect lists of marketing and PR teams, wrote tailored outreach, negotiated rates and delivered campaign briefs and post-campaign reports.

  3. Shaped content for partnerships

    I advised the creators on formats, recurring segments and topics that attract brands, so organic content and paid work reinforce each other.

What changed

  • 3x

    Rate per sponsored post BRL 1K → 3K+ (3x)

  • 10+

    10+ brand deals, around BRL 50K in revenue

  • +37%

    Combined audience 160K → 220K (+37%) in under a year

What I learned

Negotiation is storytelling with numbers. It's the same skill that works in-house: a clear brief and a solid report are what get leadership to say yes. I also learned that a brand needs to see itself in the content. That's why I started suggesting new formats that would attract both brands and followers.
