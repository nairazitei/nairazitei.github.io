About me

I'm a Brazilian journalist who found her place in corporate communications.

I started in newsrooms (g1, CNN Brasil, Record TV), where deadlines taught me to verify fast and write clearly. When I moved to internal communications, first as an intern at Bosch and later for Auren Energia, I realised the job was the same. It's about telling stories and translating complex subjects in a way that makes people interested, always grounded in data and with an eye on communication trends.

In 2026 I finished a postgraduate specialisation in Strategic Corporate Communication and Crisis Management at FAAP, and a few months earlier I moved to Switzerland with a one-way ticket. I'm learning French, rebuilding my routine and looking for an in-house team where communication is treated as a strategic function.

Let's talk

I'm open to communications and content roles in Switzerland, on-site in Geneva, Lausanne or Valais, hybrid or remote.
