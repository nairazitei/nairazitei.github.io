An AI workflow that halved my weekly admin

Lumiar Agency · Side project

Managing brand partnerships meant tracking dozens of email threads and updating a prospecting spreadsheet by hand. So I built a workflow with Claude: every Monday, it reads the week's emails in Gmail, updates each brand's status in the tracker and hands me a document with who to follow up and who to contact next. It cut the time I spend on this admin in half, and gave that time back to strategy and outreach.

The same idea works in-house: newsletter reporting, demand intake or media monitoring are all mechanical work that AI can take on, so communicators can focus on the part that needs judgement.
